# Property Guidelines

These rules apply to the property section of the package. Measure all ages from the submission date. TIV means total insured value: building + contents + business income, per location.

## PROP-1: Total insured value (TIV) per location

- Up to $3,000,000: acceptable.
- More than $3,000,000 and up to $5,000,000: REFER.
- More than $5,000,000: DECLINE.

## PROP-2: Building age

Applies only when we insure the building.

- Built in 1970 or later: acceptable.
- Built before 1970: acceptable only if the electrical, plumbing, heating and roof systems were **all** updated within the past 25 years. If any one of them was not, REFER.

## PROP-3: Construction type

- Masonry, joisted masonry, or noncombustible construction: acceptable.
- Frame (wood) construction: acceptable when the location's TIV is $1,000,000 or less. Above $1,000,000: REFER.

## PROP-4: Roof age

Applies only when we insure the building.

- 15 years or less: acceptable.
- 16 to 20 years: acceptable only with a roof inspection report dated within the past 2 years; otherwise REFER.
- More than 20 years: REFER.

## PROP-5: Cooking and fire protection

- Commercial cooking that produces grease-laden vapors (deep fryers, flat-top grills, char-broilers, woks) requires a UL-300 hood fire-suppression system serviced within the past 6 months. If it is missing or not serviced: REFER.
- Espresso machines, ovens, toasters, panini presses and microwaves do not count as grease-producing cooking.
- Buildings larger than 25,000 sq ft must have automatic sprinklers. If not sprinklered: REFER.

## PROP-6: Flood zone

- FEMA flood zone X (or B or C): acceptable.
- FEMA flood zone A, AE, AO, AH, V or VE: REFER.

## PROP-7: Occupancy

If less than 50% of the building is occupied: DECLINE.
