# Appetite and Eligibility

- **Program:** Main Street Business Package (property + general liability) for small businesses
- **Insurer:** Northwind Mutual (fictional, for testing only)

Each rule has an ID. Every underwriting decision must cite the rule IDs it relies on.

## APP-1: Eligible business classes

We write these classes when all other guidelines are met:

- Cafés, coffee shops, bakeries and restaurants
- Retail stores: clothing, hardware, books, gifts, and groceries (grocery stores under 10,000 sq ft only)
- Professional offices (accountants, lawyers, consultants, insurance agents, architects)
- Hair and beauty salons
- Artisan contractors doing residential or light commercial work: carpentry, handyman, painting, drywall, flooring, deck building

## APP-2: Ineligible business classes (DECLINE)

Decline any application in these classes:

- Bars, lounges or nightclubs where alcohol is more than 50% of revenue
- Cannabis growing, processing or sales
- Firearms or ammunition manufacturing or sales
- Fireworks or explosives
- Roofing contractors (roofing work is more than 25% of revenue)
- Demolition contractors
- Vacant or mostly unoccupied buildings (see PROP-7)

## APP-3: Classes not listed

A business class that is not listed in APP-1 or APP-2 must be REFERRED.

## APP-4: Years in business

- 2 years or more: acceptable.
- Less than 2 years: REFER.

## APP-5: Territory

We only write businesses whose operations and insured locations are in the United States. Any location outside the United States: DECLINE.
