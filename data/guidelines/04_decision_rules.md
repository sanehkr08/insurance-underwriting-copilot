# Decision Rules and Underwriting Authority

These rules say how to turn the guideline checks into one decision.

## AUTH-1: Decline first

If any rule says DECLINE, the decision is **DECLINE**, even if other rules say REFER or acceptable.

## AUTH-2: Then refer

If no rule says DECLINE, but any rule says REFER, the decision is **REFER** (send to a senior underwriter).

If any information in AUTH-5 is missing or unclear, the decision is also **REFER**, and the missing items must be listed.

## AUTH-3: Otherwise accept

If no rule says DECLINE or REFER and no required information is missing, the decision is **ACCEPT**.

## AUTH-4: Cite the rules, don't invent them

- Every reason must cite the rule ID it comes from (for example "PROP-4").
- Use only the rules in these guidelines. Do not apply rules that are not written here.
- Quote the facts from the application that triggered each rule.

## AUTH-5: Required information

Every application must include:

1. Business name and a description of operations (business class)
2. Years in business
3. Annual revenue
4. Location address and FEMA flood zone
5. Total insured value: building (if insured), contents, business income
6. Loss history for the past 3 years (or a statement that there were no claims)
7. Whether any insurer cancelled or non-renewed coverage in the past 3 years

Also required in these cases:

- **If the building is insured:** year built, construction type, roof age, and the update years for electrical, plumbing and heating if built before 1970
- **Restaurants, cafés and bars:** alcohol share of revenue and type of cooking equipment
- **Contractors:** breakdown of work by type, maximum working height, share of work subcontracted, and whether certificates of insurance are collected from subcontractors

Roof age and building details are **not** required when the applicant rents the space and does not insure the building.
