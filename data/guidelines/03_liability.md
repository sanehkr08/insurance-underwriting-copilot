# General Liability Guidelines

These rules apply to the general liability section of the package.

## GL-1: Annual revenue

- Up to $5,000,000: acceptable.
- More than $5,000,000 and up to $10,000,000: REFER.
- More than $10,000,000: DECLINE.

## GL-2: Alcohol sales

Measured as a share of total annual revenue.

- 25% or less: acceptable.
- More than 25% and up to 50%: REFER.
- More than 50%: DECLINE (see also APP-2).

Planned future alcohol sales do not trigger this rule, but note them for the underwriter.

## GL-3: Loss history

Count all property and liability claims in the past 3 years, open or closed. "Total incurred" is paid plus reserved.

- 0 or 1 claim **and** total incurred under $50,000: acceptable.
- 2 or 3 claims, **or** total incurred from $50,000 up to $150,000: REFER.
- 4 or more claims, **or** total incurred over $150,000: DECLINE.

## GL-4: Subcontractors (contractors only)

- Subcontracted work of 25% or less of revenue: acceptable.
- More than 25% and up to 50%: acceptable only if the applicant collects certificates of insurance from **every** subcontractor. If not: REFER.
- More than 50%: DECLINE.

## GL-5: Working height (contractors only)

Any work above 3 stories: DECLINE.

## GL-6: Prior cancellation or non-renewal

If another insurer cancelled or declined to renew the applicant's coverage in the past 3 years for any reason other than the insurer leaving the market: REFER.
